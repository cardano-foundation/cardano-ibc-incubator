The qualified local/devnet lifecycle population has two channels, two holders, two escrow shards and eight authenticated objects. A production-size downtime SLA, larger real-network populations, worst-case receive budget headroom and final acceptance of newly changed compiled artifacts remain release qualifications. No mainnet or real user funds were used.

Only compatible replacement of the five spending roles is supported. Kernel/NFT policies, original voucher/state policies and identities, proof/session/recovery/trace dependencies and commitment encodings stay immutable. Arbitrary state/schema/root rewriting, generic channel upgrade and replacement routes/assets are rejected. Governance that approves arbitrary successor logic may authorize later theft; conservation during handover does not remove that trust. No live immutable deployment exists here, and no retroactive recovery claim is made.
